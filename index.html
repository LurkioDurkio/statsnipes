🎯 How to Play STATSNIPES

        
      


      

        

          
🔥 Mode 1: Top 100 Survival

          
    
            
  • The Goal: Name players who rank in the Top 100 for the active NBA, NFL, or MLB statistical category.
    • 
            
  • Rank = Points: You earn points equal to the player's exact rank on the list!
              
      
                
    • Guessing #1 (e.g., LeBron James in Career Points) gives you just 1 point.
      • 
                
    • Guessing #99 (e.g., Chris Bosh) gives you 99 points!
      • 
              
    
            
    • 
            
  • 3 Lives (Strikes): Everyone starts with 3 Lives ❤️❤️❤️. If you guess a player who is outside the Top 100 (like #101 Grant Hill!), you score 0 points and lose a life 💔!
    • 
            
  • Winning: Play solo for a high score or pass-and-play with 2–4 friends until everyone is out of lives or one player is the last standing!
    • 
          

        


        

          
⚡ Mode 2: Target Stat Total (Closest Without Busting)

          
    
            
  • The Goal: You are given a Target Stat Total (e.g., 115,000 Career NBA Points or 2,500 Career MLB Home Runs).
    • 
            
  • Draft Your Lineup: Over 5 picks, lock in players whose real career stats add up as close to the target number as possible.
    • 
            
  • Don't Bust: Under standard Blackjack rules, going over the target total busts that pick!
